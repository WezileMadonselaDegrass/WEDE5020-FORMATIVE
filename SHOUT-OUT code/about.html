Our Beginning

The SHOUT OUT Story

SHOUT OUT was founded on a simple yet profound belief: modern families deserve premium protection for their increasingly digital lives. In today's connected world, households rely on countless devices, online accounts, home networks, cloud services and digital communication channels to live, work and connect.

We recognised that while digital technology has brought unprecedented convenience and opportunity, it has also introduced new vulnerabilities that many families are not equipped to handle. Traditional cybersecurity solutions were often too technical, too impersonal or simply not designed with the family unit in mind.

SHOUT OUT exists to bridge this gap. We bring professional-grade cybersecurity into the home, making it accessible, understandable and trustworthy for every member of the family. Our approach combines technical excellence with genuine care—because we believe that protecting families means more than just installing software; it means providing peace of mind.
